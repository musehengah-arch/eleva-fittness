ELEVA FITNESS — Website Package
================================
"A Healthy Mind in a Healthy Body"

HOW TO USE
----------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere on your computer.
2. Double-click  index.html  — it opens in any modern browser
   (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). No server or internet needed,
   except the Google Map on the Location tab.

WHAT'S INSIDE
-------------
index.html      — the full one-page website (all tabs/sections)
css/style.css   — the yellow / purple / blue design theme
js/script.js    — bookings, daily specialist rota, reviews & ratings
images/         — 12 royalty-free photos used across the site

FEATURES
--------
* Services & prices in Kenya Shillings (Ksh), incl. all 11 massages
* Gym & aerobics session times: 5–8 AM, 12–2 PM, 6–10 PM daily
* Daily specialists: pick any date + service to see the 3 on duty
* Booking form — requests are saved in the visitor's browser
  (localStorage) and listed under "Your Bookings"
* Team page: doctors, massage therapists & gym trainers with qualifications
* Reviews: visitors can rate (1–5 stars) and write testimonials;
  new reviews appear instantly and persist in the browser
* Location tab with embedded Google Map of Westlands, Nairobi
* Social follow buttons + email: elevafittness@gmail.com

TO PUT IT ONLINE (free options)
-------------------------------
* GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop (drag the folder in), or Vercel.
* For the booking form to reach your email inbox, connect a free
  form service such as Formspree or Web3Forms (replace the form's
  submit handler in js/script.js with their endpoint).

NOTE: Social media links currently point to placeholder handles
(facebook.com/elevafitness etc.) — update them to your real pages
in index.html (search for "facebook.com/elevafitness").
